What to Expect.

We’ll guide you through each stage of your reconstruction project, from the initial assessment to the final walkthrough.

  1. Initial Inspection & AssessmentWe evaluate the property, document repairs, and discuss your goals.
  2. Estimate & Scope ReviewWe explain the proposed work, materials, and next steps.
  3. Work AuthorizationReview and sign the applicable attorney-approved agreements before work begins.
  4. Scheduling & Material SelectionsWe coordinate the schedule, trades, and your material choices.
  5. Reconstruction BeginsOur team completes approved repairs and communicates progress or changes.
  6. Final Walkthrough & CompletionWe review the completed work and address applicable punch-list items.
